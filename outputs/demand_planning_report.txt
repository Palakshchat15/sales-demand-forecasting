Executive Summary:

As we approach the end of 2025, it's essential to review our sales demand forecasts for the next 90 days. Our in-house models, Prophet, LightGBM, and Seasonal Naive, have provided us with forecasts for each product category. This report summarizes the key findings from these models and provides recommendations for our pipeline.

Model Performance & Recommendation:

Our models have performed as follows:

* Model accuracy was measured on a past backtest window (2025-10-03 to 2025-12-31)
* Mean holdout MAPE (simple average of the 8 categories, lower is better): Seasonal Naive 12.37%, Prophet 12.40%, LightGBM 14.90%
* Seasonal Naive has the lowest mean error, 0.03 percentage points below Prophet, so on this holdout the two are effectively tied.
* The forward forecast in this report (2026-01-01 to 2026-03-31) comes from Prophet, the only model the pipeline currently uses for forward forecasts.
* Recommendation: No model clearly wins. Keep Prophet for forward forecasts for now, keep Seasonal Naive as the benchmark every model must beat, and do not switch models over a gap this small.
