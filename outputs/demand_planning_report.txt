Model Performance & Recommendation:

Our model performance analysis shows that:

- Seasonal Naive has the lowest mean holdout MAPE (12.37%), making it a strong baseline for comparison.
- Prophet has a mean holdout MAPE of 12.40%, which is only slightly higher than Seasonal Naive.
- LightGBM has a mean holdout MAPE of 14.90%, which is significantly higher than the other two models.
- No model clearly outperforms the others, and we recommend keeping Prophet for forward forecasts for now.
- We will continue to monitor the performance of our models and consider updates or changes as necessary.
